Who we are

FlowPOS is run by Flow POS Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16246833), at Unit 2 Dudley Hill Business Park, Rook Lane, Bradford, West Yorkshire, BD4 9NU, United Kingdom.

We provide point-of-sale (EPOS) software, online stores, payments, inventory tools and related apps to businesses (“merchants”).

Our role depends on who you are

Data protection law gives different jobs to different organisations. Here is how that works at FlowPOS:

If you are a merchant

FlowPOS is the controller of your account data. We decide how it is used, and this policy explains it.

If you shop with a merchant

The merchant is the controller of your order data. FlowPOS is their processor – we handle it only on their instructions.

So if you bought something from a shop that uses FlowPOS, the shop is your main contact for privacy questions. Their own privacy policy also applies. We still explain below what happens to your data on our systems.

Data we collect from merchants

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number, job role, login details.
  • Business details: business name, address, company number, VAT number, bank details for payouts.
  • Identity checks: ID documents and ownership information, needed by our payment partners for “know your customer” (KYC) checks.
  • Staff data: names, PINs and roles of staff you add to your tills.
  • Usage data: log-ins, device and browser information, IP address, actions taken in the dashboard.
  • Support messages: what you send us by email, chat or phone.
  • Billing: your subscription plan and payment history with us.

Data we handle for shoppers

When you buy from a merchant in-store or through their FlowPOS online store, we may process the following on the merchant’s behalf:

  • Contact details: name, email, phone number.
  • Delivery details: delivery and billing address.
  • Order details: what you bought, when, prices, discounts and order notes.
  • Customer account: login details and saved addresses, if you create an account with the store.
  • Loyalty data: points or rewards, if the merchant uses them.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited on the store.

The merchant decides why this data is used (for example, to deliver your order or send receipts). If you want to see, correct or delete it, please contact the merchant first. If you contact us, we will pass your request to them.

We also use some of this data as a controller, only to keep the platform secure and prevent fraud (legitimate interests).

Payments

Card payments are handled by regulated payment providers, including Stripe, Adyen, Ryft and Transactworld. Card details go straight to these providers. FlowPOS does not store full card numbers or security codes.

We may receive limited payment information back, such as the card type, the last four digits, and whether the payment succeeded. Each provider is responsible for the data it holds and has its own privacy policy.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We only share it with:

  • Hosting and infrastructure: Microsoft Azure (UK South) and Cloudflare (network security and delivery).
  • Payment providers: Stripe, Adyen, Ryft and Transactworld, to take payments and verify merchants.
  • Analytics providers: Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without using cookies.
  • Apps a merchant installs: if a merchant connects a third-party app from our marketplace, that app gets only the data the merchant approves.
  • Professional advisers: lawyers, accountants and auditors, when needed.
  • Authorities: police, regulators or courts, when the law requires it.
  • A buyer of our business: if FlowPOS is sold or merged, data may move to the new owner under the same protections.

All our suppliers are bound by contracts that require them to protect personal data.

Where your data is kept

Our main servers are in the UK (Microsoft Azure, UK South region).

Some suppliers, and some of our own team members, may access data from outside the UK. When this happens, we protect it with approved safeguards, such as UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

How we keep data safe

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Each merchant’s data is kept separate from other merchants’ data.
  • Staff access is limited to people who need it for their job.
  • We monitor our systems and keep software up to date.

No system is 100% secure. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to:

  • Access – get a copy of your data.
  • Correct – fix data that is wrong.
  • Delete – remove your data, in some cases.
  • Restrict – pause how we use it, in some cases.
  • Object – stop us using it for legitimate interests or marketing.
  • Port – get your data in a format you can move elsewhere.
  • Withdraw consent – at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use these rights, email us (see below). We usually reply within one month. It is normally free.

Shoppers: please contact the shop you bought from first, as they control your data. We will help them answer you.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would like the chance to fix things first, so please contact us.

Children

FlowPOS is a service for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 as our own customers. Merchants are responsible for any age rules that apply to their own shoppers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top. If the changes are important, we will tell merchants by email or in the dashboard.
