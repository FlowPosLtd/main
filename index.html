FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Get in touch.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. FlowPOS has no monthly fee. You pay per transaction: 1.39% in person and 1.25% + 20p online on UK consumer cards.

What about business cards, Amex and other card types?

Other card types: contact us and we will talk you through the rates for your business.

Do I need a card terminal?

Not to get started. You can take contactless payments with Tap to Pay on iPhone in the FlowPOS app, send payment links, or use a card terminal with FlowPOS EPOS at your counter.

Can I sell online as well as in person?

Yes. Build your store with the AI Web Builder, take payments with online checkout, and see online and in-person sales in one dashboard.

Is FlowPOS a bank?

No. Flow Banking, including business accounts, same-day settlement and multi-currency, is provided through our regulated partners. FlowPOS is not a bank.

Do I have to use stablecoin payouts?

No. Stablecoin payouts are optional and available only where permitted by local regulations.

How do I get started?

Create your account at app.flowpos.co.uk, or leave your details below and our team will contact you.
